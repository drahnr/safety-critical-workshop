# `goal`: Goals for the *RAD* Product

This section lists the high level goals for the *RAD* product, a device for radiation therapy.

## `goal.kill-cancer`: Kill malignant cells

The *RAD* product must be able to kill malignant human cells, or at least control the growth of such cells.
Without killing malignant cells, they may spread to other parts of a human body, which often results in early death.

## `goal.EU-MDR-compliant`: Compliance with EU regulations

- **Manual Verification:** true

The *RAD* product must be compliant with the [EU-MDR](https://eumdr.com) to sell *RAD* in the EU.
